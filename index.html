1Welcome

Iqama Compass turns scattered Iqama advice into one personal plan. You enter your situation once, and the app ranks your routes, estimates your timeline and costs, tests your family plan, and prints a branded report.

Who this app is for

  • Individual professionals outside Saudi Arabia who want to know which work route fits their degree, experience, salary, and capital.
  • Families deciding whether and when to bring a spouse and children, and what it costs each year.
  • Entrepreneurs and investors comparing sponsor-free Premium Residency with building a licensed Saudi business.
  • Coaches and consultants who need a client-ready report. Section 8 shows how to run a session with it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not file anything with a government, and it does not connect to Qiwa, Absher, or any official system.
  • It does not give legal or immigration advice, and its scores do not predict an official decision.
  • It does not cover student residency. It covers work-based residency only.
  • It does not sync between devices. Your answers live in the browser you use (Chapter 4).

The Creed-to-Commerce™ stages

Everything in the app is organized around six stages. Each stage is scored on your Profile, tracked on your Action Plan, and shown in your report. The percentage is how much that stage counts toward your overall readiness score.

1. Creed

Purpose, principles, and family agreement.

10% of overall score

2. Calling

Skills, sector, and market position.

20% of overall score

3. Credentials

Degrees, accreditation, and clearances.

25% of overall score

4. Contract

Offer, Qiwa contract, visa, and entry.

20% of overall score

5. Compliance

Iqama, Absher, renewals, and clean records.

10% of overall score

6. Commerce

Stability, Premium Residency, and legacy.

15% of overall score
Integrity guardrailsNever pay anyone for a "free visa" or a fake sponsor. Never work on a visit visa. Never put a business in a Saudi's name to hide your ownership (concealment, called tasattur). In a normal contract your employer pays the Iqama, the work-permit levy, and your health insurance. The app repeats these rules because they protect your family from fines, deportation, and entry bans.

2Quick start

Seven steps take you from opening the file to a printed report. Plan on about 30 minutes for your first pass.

  1. Open the fileOpen Iqama_Compass.html in a current desktop browser. You will see a green header, a gold banner reading SAMPLE DATA loaded, and a fictional client named Yusuf Rahman.
  2. Explore for five minutesClick each tab. Change the pathway on the Timeline tab. Open the Report tab. Nothing you do here affects anyone else.
  3. Clear the sample dataClick Clear Sample Data in the banner or the header. Every personal answer disappears. The built-in reference fees and the 31 template tasks stay.
  4. Fill in your Profile, then your householdAnswer every field you can on the Profile tab. Blank fields count as zero, so your scores rise as you add facts. Then enter your family on the Family tab.
  5. Read your resultsThe Profile tab shows your readiness wheel and your biggest gaps. The Pathways tab ranks ten routes. Click Plan this route on the one you want.
  6. Check the timeline and costsSet a start date on the Timeline tab. Review the Costs tab and edit the landing budget to match real quotes.
  7. Work the plan and printTick tasks on the Action Plan tab. When you are ready, click Print / Save as PDF and save the report.

3Installing and opening

Iqama Compass is one HTML file. There is nothing to install and no account to create. It runs in the browser on your computer, tablet, or phone.

Option A: open the file on your computer

  1. Save Iqama_Compass.html to a folder you will remember.
  2. Double-click it, or drag it into Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
  3. Bookmark it. Your answers stay with that browser and that file location.
Use the same file location each timeBrowsers tie saved answers to the address of the page. If you move the file, or open a copy from a different folder or website, you may see an empty app. Keep one master copy and bookmark it.

Option B: host it for clients (GitHub to Vercel)

This matches the way you publish other BMPA apps.

  1. Create a GitHub repository, for example iqama-compass.
  2. Add the file and rename it index.html so it loads at the site root.
  3. In Vercel, import the repository and deploy. No build command or framework is needed.
  4. Share the Vercel address. Each visitor's answers stay in their own browser and never reach your server.
Updating the app laterWhen a fee or rule changes, edit the file, commit it, and Vercel redeploys. Visitors keep their saved answers because the page address does not change.

Requirements

  • A current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
  • An internet connection the first time, to load the two typefaces (Amiri and Nunito Sans). Offline, the app falls back to Georgia and your system font, and everything still works.
  • A desktop browser for the best printing. Phones can use every tab, but printing options vary by device.
Printing does not work in embedded previewsChat windows, file previews, and some email viewers block the print dialog. If Print / Save as PDF does nothing, open the file directly in a browser tab.

4Your data and privacy

Sample data versus your data

The first time you open the app it loads a fictional client, Yusuf Rahman, so you can see every screen working. The gold banner and the words SAMPLE DATA on the report cover mark it clearly. Once you clear it, the banner disappears and the app treats everything as yours.

The three header buttons
ButtonWhat it does
Print / Save as PDFJumps to the Report tab and opens your browser's print dialog. See Chapter 7.
Clear Sample DataWhile sample data is loaded, empties every personal answer in one click. After you have your own data, the same button reads Erase My Data and needs two clicks (see below).
Load Sample DataBrings back the fictional client. If you have your own data, the first click changes the label to Click again to replace my data. A second click within four seconds replaces your answers with the sample.

What clearing removes

  • Every Profile answer and check box
  • Your household on the Family tab
  • Selected Vision 2030 contributions
  • The chosen pathway, start date, and cost horizon
  • Task ticks, due dates, and custom tasks (the template list returns, unticked)
  • Notes
  • Landing-budget figures (reset to the starting estimates)

What clearing keeps

  • The Prepared by name on the Report tab
  • The built-in reference fees, thresholds, and rules
  • The 31 template tasks
  • The ten pathway descriptions and the Background tab

Where your answers are saved

The app saves automatically after every change, inside your browser's own storage (local storage). Nothing is sent over the internet. The consequences are practical:

  • Answers appear only in the same browser, on the same device, at the same page address.
  • A private or incognito window starts empty and forgets everything when closed.
  • Clearing your browser's site data deletes your answers. There is no recovery.
  • There is no export file. To keep a record, print the report to PDF (Chapter 7).
Shared or public computersClick Erase My Data before you leave, then click it again within four seconds to confirm. The two-click step prevents accidents.

Working with more than one client

The app holds one profile at a time. A coach has three safe options:

  1. Print the PDF at the end of each session, then erase and start the next client.
  2. Give each client a separate browser profile (for example, a Chrome profile per client).
  3. Let clients use their own copy on their own device, with you reviewing their printed report.

Reading the results panel

  • Overall score and band. 75 or more is Launch-ready, 55 to 74 Nearly ready, 35 to 54 Build phase, and below 35 Foundation phase.
  • Likely skill band. A coaching estimate of High-Skill, Skilled, or Basic, modeled on the factors the skills-based work-permit system weighs. Employers' real classification can differ.

  • Biggest gaps. Up to six specific actions, ordered from your weakest stage upward.

5.3 Pathways

Purpose: compare ten work-based routes and pick one to plan.

  • Fit ranking. Every route scored 0 to 100 with a colored bar. Scores show zero until you enter a profile.
  • Lanes. Lane A: Enter routes get you your first work Iqama. Lane B: Anchor routes make residency durable and less dependent on one employer. Lane C: Family widens it to your household.
  • Route cards. Each card gives a summary, Longevity and Sponsor independence ratings (one to five bars), who pays, the cost to hold the status, the official channel, step-by-step instructions, and watch-outs. Open the two folding sections to read them.
  • Track pill. On Premium Talent, a pill names the track you come closest to: Healthcare, Science and Research, or Executive.
  • Plan this route. Sets the pathway and takes you to the Timeline tab.
Fit is a coaching indexIt combines published thresholds with coaching judgment. A low fit tells you what to build, not that you are barred. A high fit does not promise approval.

5.4 Timeline

Purpose: turn a route into dates.

  1. Pick a pathway (or arrive here through Plan this route).
  2. Choose a start date. If you leave it blank the app uses today.
  3. Read the four tiles: best-case finish, likely finish, slow-case finish, and the number of phases.
  • Each row shows minimum / likely / maximum weeks and the dates the likely span covers. The solid bar is the likely duration and the faint line extending from it is the slow case. The month axis runs along the top.
  • The app places phases one after another. That is cautious, because some real steps overlap, such as clearances while you attest degrees.
  • The ten-year horizon table at the bottom links the first Iqama to later milestones: Year 0, Year 1, Years 2 to 4, Years 3 to 7, and Year 5 onward.

5.5 Costs

Purpose: show what it costs to arrive, to stay, and to become permanent.

  • Four tiles. Landing capital needed, sponsored fees per year for your household, Premium Annual (SAR 100,000 a year), and Premium Permanent (SAR 800,000 once). Each shows the dollar equivalent at SAR 3.75.
  • Cumulative chart. Three lines over 5, 10, 15, or 20 years: sponsored fees, Annual, and Permanent. Use the Horizon menu to change the span. A dashed marker at year 8 shows where Annual overtakes the one-time Permanent fee. Real estate is left out because the money stays in the property.
  • Insight boxes. Plain-language readings of your numbers, including how many years of sponsored fees equal the Permanent fee and how much of the landing capital your savings cover.
  • Landing budget. Eight editable figures: degree attestation, police clearance, medical exam, flights and relocation, housing deposit, accreditation, family documents, and the family visa fee. Overtype any figure with a real quote. Totals and runway months update below.
What you can and cannot editYou can edit the eight landing-budget figures here and the insurance amount per dependent on the Family tab. The statutory rates (for example the SAR 800 monthly levy, the SAR 400 monthly dependent fee, and the Premium fees) are built into the calculations. If a rule changes, update the file before relying on the totals.

6.3 Cost formulas

  • Eligible dependents = spouse (1 if Yes) + sons under 18 + unmarried daughters.
  • Sponsored fees per year = SAR 650 (Iqama) + SAR 9,600 (work-permit levy, SAR 800 a month) + SAR 4,800 for each eligible dependent (SAR 400 a month).
  • Dependent fees in year 1 = dependents × SAR 400 × 9 months, because new arrivals are reported exempt for the first 90 days.
  • Family year 1 = year-1 dependent fees + insurance + family visa fee + family document costs (the last two apply only if you have eligible dependents).
  • Household per month = housing + other costs + schooling ÷ 12 + (annual dependent fees + insurance) ÷ 12.
  • Landing capital = the six arrival items (attestation, police clearance, medical, flights, housing deposit, accreditation) + family year 1.
  • Premium Annual = SAR 100,000 for each year. Permanent = SAR 800,000 once. At eight years Annual equals the Permanent fee, and after that it costs more.
  • Dollar figures divide riyals by 3.75.

10Troubleshooting and FAQ

The Print button does nothing.

Printing is blocked inside previews, chat windows, and some embedded viewers. Open the file in a normal tab of Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox and try again. If a pop-up blocker is active, allow it for this file.

My PDF has no colors or looks plain.

Turn on Background graphics (Chrome and Edge) or Print backgrounds (Firefox and Safari) in the print dialog, then save again.

My answers vanished.

Answers live in the browser's local storage for one page address. They disappear if you used a private window, cleared site data, switched browsers or devices, or opened the file from a different folder or web address. Print a PDF after important sessions so a record exists outside the browser.

Every route shows a fit of zero.

The profile is empty. Enter at least one of degree, experience, salary, sector, or name, and the scores appear. They rise as you complete more fields.

Why does Premium Talent score only 10?

10 is the floor. The Talent tracks need specific facts: healthcare plus completed accreditation and a master's, a doctorate with a salary of SAR 14,000 or more in an education sector, or a C-suite role at SAR 80,000 a month or more. Until you meet one, the score stays low.

The Family tab is empty.

It shows results once you set a spouse answer or enter dependents, sons, parents, or housing. Choose No in Spouse to sponsor if you travel alone and want to see the other results.

Can I change the levy, dependent fee, or Premium fees?

Not on screen in this edition. You can edit the eight landing-budget figures and the insurance amount per dependent. The statutory rates are built into the calculations. If a rule changes, update the file before relying on the totals, and note that in client reports.

The Timeline start date is blank. Is that a problem?

No. The app counts from today when you leave it blank. Set a real date to get meaningful calendar dates.

Which pathway should I pick?

Use the ranking as a conversation starter. Pick one route from Lane A to get in and one from Lane B to build toward. Check the watch-outs and confirm requirements on the official channel named on the card before you act.

Is there an Arabic version?

The app is in English. Arabic appears only in the Basmala at the top of the app and the report. A translated edition would need new text throughout.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Every tab adapts to a narrow screen and wide tables scroll sideways. Printing from a phone depends on the device, so a desktop browser is best for reports.

Can two people work on the same plan?

Not at the same time. There is no sync. Pass the printed PDF, or share one device.

Does the app submit anything to the government?

No. It is a planning and coaching tool. All applications go through the official channels: Qiwa, Absher, Muqeem, MOFA, MISA, and the Premium Residency Center.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is educational coaching material. Rules change, and several figures come from secondary guides. Confirm every requirement officially and consult a qualified adviser for your own case.

I clicked Erase My Data by mistake.

The first click only changes the label. Data is erased only by a second click within four seconds. If it was erased, the data cannot be recovered, so reload the sample or re-enter from your last printed report.

11Glossary

Iqama
The residence permit issued to foreigners living in Saudi Arabia.
Absher
The Ministry of Interior's online service for exit and re-entry visas, family visas, and Iqama renewals.
Muqeem
The online service where residents and employers check Iqama records. Also the name of the resident ID card.
Qiwa
The labor-market platform where employers register contracts and manage work permits.
MHRSD or HRSD
The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, which regulates work permits and Saudization.
MOFA
The Ministry of Foreign Affairs. It authorizes visas and attests documents.
MISA
The Ministry of Investment. It licenses foreign investors and endorses Investor residency.
Monsha'at
The authority that supports small and medium enterprises and startups.
Premium Residency Center
The body that issues Premium Residency, reached at pr.gov.sa.
Nitaqat
The program that rates employers by their share of Saudi staff and sets service levels such as Platinum, Green, Yellow, and Red. The 2026 to 2028 phase aims to localize more than 340,000 additional private-sector jobs.
Saudization
Policy that reserves and prioritizes jobs for Saudi nationals, with quotas by sector.
Mehna
The profession recorded on your Iqama. It must match your real job.
Skill band
The High-Skill, Skilled, and Basic classification of work permits based on education, experience, skills, wage, and age.
Work-permit levy
The monthly fee an employer pays for each foreign worker, reported at SAR 800.
Dependent fee
The monthly fee for each sponsored family member, reported at SAR 400.
GOSI
The General Organization for Social Insurance. Employees are registered with it.
SCFHS
The Saudi Commission for Health Specialties, which recognizes health practitioners.
KACST
King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology, a possible endorser for the Science and Research Talent track.
REGA
The General Real Estate Authority, which accredits property valuations and designates zones for foreign ownership.
Watheeq
The title-deed system used to prove property ownership.
Jawazat
The passport authority that processes Iqama issuance and biometrics.
Wasil and National Address
Your registered address and the postal service that delivers the Iqama card to it.
Tasattur
Concealment. Hiding true ownership of a business behind a nominee, which is an offence.
Lane A, B, C
This app's grouping of routes: A enters, B anchors, and C widens residency to the family.
Fit score
The app's 0 to 100 coaching index for a route.
Landing capital
The cash you need to arrive and settle: arrival costs plus the family's first year.
Runway
How many months your savings cover your household costs.
Vision 2030
Saudi Arabia's national transformation plan, launched in 2016 around a vibrant society, a thriving economy, and an ambitious nation.
Creed-to-Commerce™
The BMPA framework in which commercial activity begins in a creed and stays accountable to it. Here it organizes the plan into six stages.

BTemplate task list

The 31 tasks that load on the Action Plan tab. Delete or add tasks to suit each client, or restore this list at any time.

1. Creed (5 tasks)

  1. Write your purpose statement. Why Saudi Arabia, why now, and whom the move serves. Aim for 3 to 5 sentences.
  2. Hold a family consultation (shura). Agree on timing, schooling, housing, and the fallback if the plan fails.
  3. Set a 10-year horizon. Decide whether you aim for a stay of 3 years, 7 years, or permanence.
  4. Sign the integrity pledge. No paid visas, no work on a visit visa, no concealed ownership.
  5. Build a six-month runway budget. Include flights, deposits, and a gap before the first salary.

2. Calling (5 tasks)

  1. Match your profession to the Saudi classification. Check that your title fits an eligible profession and the skills-based band.
  2. Choose two Vision 2030 sectors. Pick sectors where your skill is scarce (see the Vision 2030 tab).
  3. Prepare a Saudi-ready CV and LinkedIn profile. Show results, certifications, and mentoring of local talent.
  4. Build a list of 30 target employers. Mix direct employers, contractors, and recruiters you have vetted.
  5. Start Arabic basics. Greetings, workplace phrases, and numbers raise trust fast.

3. Credentials (6 tasks)

  1. Check passport validity. At least 6 months for the visa and 90 days for exit and re-entry.
  2. Start the degree attestation chain. Home authentication, Saudi embassy, and MOFA as required. Often the longest step.
  3. Order police clearance. Keep originals and certified translations ready.
  4. Begin professional accreditation if regulated. SCFHS, engineering, accounting, or your own professional body.
  5. Book your medical check. Use an approved center; results can expire, so time it to the visa.
  6. Attest marriage and birth certificates. Needed for family sponsorship. Allow several weeks.

4. Contract (5 tasks)

  1. Get the offer in writing. Role, profession on the Iqama, salary, term, housing, flights, and family benefits.
  2. Confirm contract is registered on Qiwa. The registered profession must match your real job.
  3. Confirm the employer pays Iqama, levy, and insurance. You should not pay for your own visa or levy.
  4. Collect the visa stamp. At a Saudi embassy or consulate once MOFA approves.
  5. Travel inside the visa window. Book flights only after the stamp is in your passport.

5. Compliance (6 tasks)

  1. Finish medical, fingerprints, and Iqama in 90 days. The 90-day window starts on arrival.
  2. Activate Absher and Muqeem. Check expiry dates yourself every month.
  3. Set expiry alerts at 90, 60, and 30 days. Do not rely only on the employer's calendar.
  4. Register your National Address. The card is delivered by post to that address.
  5. Confirm health insurance and GOSI registration. Insurance is a condition of renewal.
  6. Sponsor your family once your Iqama has 3+ months left. Use Absher with the company-attested documents.

6. Commerce (4 tasks)

  1. Review transfer freedom at month 12. Qualifying workers can transfer without sponsor consent after one year.
  2. Choose a Premium Residency category. Talent, Annual, Permanent, Real estate, Investor, or Entrepreneur.
  3. Build the capital plan for your target category. Annual SAR 100,000 a year, Permanent SAR 800,000, Real estate SAR 4,000,000.
  4. Document your contribution to Vision 2030. Training delivered, jobs created, exports, patents, projects.
